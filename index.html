How I work 我的工作方式

From field problem to working system. 从业务现场到可运行系统。

I work with customer teams to find high-leverage workflows, prototype agentic solutions, and turn what we learn into reliable, repeatable deployments. 我与客户团队一起识别高价值工作流、验证 Agent 方案，并把现场经验沉淀为可靠、可复用的交付。

01

Field discovery现场需求发现

Map real workflows with users and stakeholders, define success criteria, and find where AI can create meaningful leverage.与用户和业务方梳理真实流程、定义成功标准，找到 AI 能创造实际价值的位置。

02

Agent deliveryAgent 方案交付

Connect models, tools, data, and interfaces—then evaluate, harden, and prepare the system for real-world rollout.连接模型、工具、数据与界面，并通过评测与工程加固，让系统进入真实业务。

03

Product feedback产品反馈闭环

Turn deployment friction and model failures into reusable patterns, clearer products, and sharper roadmap decisions.把交付阻力与模型失败沉淀为可复用模式、产品改进与更清晰的路线判断。
