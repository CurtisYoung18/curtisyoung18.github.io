A working POC that helps investigation teams turn an incident brief into a bilingual questionnaire, collect public responses through shareable forms, and analyze the resulting evidence with an AI assistant.一套可运行 POC：帮助调查团队把事件简报转成双语问卷，通过公开链接收集回复，再由 AI 助手分析调查证据。

01

Operational pressure业务压力

Public-health teams need to move from an emerging incident to structured evidence quickly, while questionnaires still require careful field design and review.公共卫生团队需要快速把突发事件转化为结构化证据，但调查问卷仍需严谨设计字段并进行人工审核。

02

Human-controlled AI人工控制的 AI

AI drafts typed bilingual questions and suggests edits; investigators retain control over fields, ordering, validation, and publication.AI 生成带字段类型的双语问题并提出修改建议；调查人员仍控制字段、顺序、校验与发布。

03

End-to-end POC端到端 POC

The prototype covers event setup, questionnaire authoring, public response collection, dashboards, and conversational response analysis.原型覆盖事件建立、问卷编辑、公众回复收集、数据看板与对话式回复分析。

Investigation flow调查流程

Move from incident brief to evidence review.从事件简报走到证据复盘。

The system treats AI as one layer in an operational workflow. It accelerates authoring and analysis without removing the investigator’s control over what is asked or published.系统把 AI 作为运营流程中的一层：加速问卷编制和数据分析，但不替代调查人员对提问与发布内容的控制。

01 · BRIEF Describe the incident录入事件简报

Create an event from its date, location, narrative, and the investigation material already available.根据日期、地点、事件描述与已有调查材料创建事件。

02 · AUTHOR Generate and edit生成并编辑问卷

GPTBots produces a bilingual field schema; investigators revise wording, types, required state, and order.GPTBots 生成双语字段 Schema；调查人员修改文案、类型、必填状态与顺序。

03 · COLLECT Publish a public form发布公开表单

A share token opens a responsive questionnaire for anonymous or identified submissions.通过分享 Token 打开响应式问卷，支持匿名或实名提交。

04 · ANALYZE Review the evidence分析调查证据

Charts summarize responses, while a streaming AI conversation examines symptoms, severity, possible causes, and follow-up actions.图表汇总回复，流式 AI 对话进一步分析症状、严重程度、可能原因与后续行动。

Reliability boundary可靠性边界

Generate a draft, never an unreviewed decision.生成草稿，而不是未经审核的决策。

The model returns a structured array of typed fields rather than free-form prose. If the AI service is unavailable or its response cannot be parsed, the API falls back to a safe default questionnaire. Investigators can then edit every field before sharing.模型返回带类型字段的结构化数组，而非自由文本；当 AI 服务不可用或结果无法解析时，API 会回退到安全的默认问卷。调查人员随后可在分享前编辑每一个字段。

Evidence synthesis证据汇总

Put response data into the conversation—not into a copy-paste task.让回复数据直接进入分析对话，而不是人工复制粘贴。

The analysis route joins event context with all questionnaire responses, formats the evidence, and streams it to the assistant. Investigators can start with a standard event review and continue asking follow-up questions in the same conversation.分析接口把事件上下文与全部问卷回复合并、格式化，并流式发送给助手。调查人员可以从标准事件分析开始，再在同一对话中继续追问。
